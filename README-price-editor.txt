LAKISHMI CRACKERS — EDITABLE PRODUCT CATALOGUE
Open price-editor.html in Chrome or Edge. Edit product names, units, selling prices and availability. Click Download updated products.js. Replace products.js in your GitHub repository and commit the change.
The tool runs locally in your browser; it does not upload changes automatically and is not password-protected. Cost prices are included for owner reference and should never be shown publicly.
The 74 draft selling prices were calculated as supplier rate × 2. Please verify OCR/transcription, pack contents, stock, taxes and legal permissions before publication. This ZIP does not activate cart, orders, payments or delivery. Confirm all applicable fireworks licensing and legal requirements before enabling sales/order functions.
